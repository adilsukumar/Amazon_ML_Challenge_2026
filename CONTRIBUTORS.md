## Attribution policy

- Each contributor authors and pushes their own work using an email connected to
  their GitHub account.
- `Co-authored-by` trailers are used only for genuine joint work and with the
  teammate’s consent.
- Reviews, experiment design, documentation, and engineering contributions are
  credited in release notes and the final methodology document.
